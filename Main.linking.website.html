Collection Made Easy

Digital Drive-Through

Order before you arrive. Collect without leaving your car. It's the future of fast food.

Step 1

Order Online

Browse the menu and place your order from anywhere — home, office, or on the road.

Step 2

Select Drive-Through

Choose "Drive-Through" at checkout and enter your vehicle registration number.

Step 3

Head to Restaurant

Drive to the restaurant at your selected collection time. No need to queue at the counter.

Step 4

Collect & Go

Give your order number or username. Staff confirms "PAID — READY" and hands over your food.
